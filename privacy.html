Privacy Policy

This page explains what personal data sourcing-sense.com collects, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: only what you type into a form, used only to answer you or send what you asked for.

Who is responsible

Sourcing Sense is run by David Račak, Olomouc, Czech Republic. Contact for anything privacy-related: info@sourcing-sense.com.

What is collected and why

WhereDataPurposeLegal basis (GDPR)
Contact formName, email, company (optional), your messageReplying to your enquirySteps prior to a contract / legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)
Resource downloads and tool results (e.g. AI policy template, AI Readiness Score)First name, work email, company (optional)Sending you the resource and at most one personal follow-upConsent, Art. 6(1)(a)
Booking a callWhatever you enter in CalendlyScheduling the callSteps prior to a contract, Art. 6(1)(b)

With every form submission the site also stores the page you sent it from and, if the link that brought you carried campaign tags (for example utm_source=linkedin) or came from another website, that source. It tells us which content is useful; it is not used to follow you elsewhere.

The free calculators and generators run in your browser. What you type into them is not sent anywhere unless you submit a form.

Who processes the data

  • Google (Apps Script and Google Sheets) stores form submissions.
  • Calendly handles call bookings under its own privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and provides Web Analytics, which does not use cookies or fingerprinting.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses.

How long data is kept

Enquiries and download records: up to 24 months after the last contact. There is no mailing list.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing, and you can withdraw consent at any time — email info@sourcing-sense.com and it will be handled within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the Czech data protection authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (uoou.gov.cz).

Cookies and browser storage

The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. The site keeps one display preference in your browser's local storage. Nothing in it identifies you.
